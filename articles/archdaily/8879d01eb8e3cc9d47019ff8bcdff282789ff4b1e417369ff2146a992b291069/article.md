*Text description provided by the architects. *Studioshaw announces the completion of Bridge House, an eight-story, 6,250 sqm mixed-use development at the heart of [Hackney Wick](/tag/hackney-wick). Delivering 40 apartments alongside dual-level commercial and community spaces, and set beside a new public square, the project marks the practice's largest scheme to date. Its completion coincides with Studioshaw's tenth anniversary and the delivery of its 100th project.
